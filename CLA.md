## How to sign

When you open your first pull request, a bot comments with a link to this document. Reply on the pull request with
exactly:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Your GitHub username and the date are recorded on the `cla-signatures` branch. You sign once; it covers all of your
future Contributions.
